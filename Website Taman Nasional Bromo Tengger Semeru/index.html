Bromo Tengger Semeru National Park is located in East Java, Indonesia, and spans across the regencies of Probolinggo, Pasuruan, Malang, and Lumajang. The park is famous for its volcanic landscapes, including Mount Bromo and Mount Semeru, the highest mountain on Java Island.

Bromo Sunset Point

Bromo Sunset Point is located within the Bromo Tengger Semeru National Park in East Java, Indonesia. Situated in the highlands surrounding Mount Bromo, this viewpoint offers panoramic views of the volcanic landscape and is a popular spot for watching the sunset over the mountains and the Sea of Sand.
