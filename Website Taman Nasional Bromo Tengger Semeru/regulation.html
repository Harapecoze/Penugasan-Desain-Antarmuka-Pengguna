PARK REGULATIONS &
VISTORS GUIDELINE

Preserving the natural beauty of Bromo Tengger Semeru National Park is a shared responsibility. Please adhere to these guidelines to ensure a safe and sustainable experience for all.

Essential Requirements

Online Ticket Purchase Required

All visitors must purchase tickets online through the official BBTNBTS website. Walk-ins are strictly prohibited and result in heavy fines.

Operating Hours
00.00 - 17.00

*Exceptions are only provided for guests with valid proof of accommodation inside the park boundaries.

Ticketing Policy

Entrance fees are set by PP No. 36 Tahun 2024. Visitors must present their official QR code at entry gates for verification.

Accommodation

Guests with confirmed bookings at hotels inside the park are permitted to enter or exit outside normal operating hours. Valid ticket and proof of reservation are required at checkpoints.

Transportation

Certified 4WD Vehicles (Jeeps) are mandatory for traversing the Tengger Caldera (Sea of Sand & Savana) areas due to safety and environmental regulations.

Drone & Photography

  • Personal drone use (non-commercial) must be booked online.
  • Commercial, pre-wedding, or professional film shoots require a SIMAKSI permit.

Entry Checklist

  • Show official QR code booking proof at gate.
  • Tour guides must possess a valid government-issued license.
  • No refunds or rescheduling permitted once booked.

Enforcement Notice

Any violation of the park regulations will result in immediate ejection from the national park, administrative fines, or legal prosecution under Indonesian law.
